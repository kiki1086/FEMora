About FEMora

FEMora is a women’s empowerment initiative by Mentor Campus that brings together passionate student leaders to build a more confident, inclusive and opportunity-driven future for women. We’re inviting enthusiastic, creative and driven female students exclusively to apply as FEMora Chapter Lead and bring this movement to their campus!
